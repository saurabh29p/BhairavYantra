## 1. Kālī: Devī Māhātmya (Durgā Saptaśatī) 7.5 / काली: दुर्गा सप्तशती ७.५

> भ्रुकुटीकुटिलात्तस्या ललाटफलकाद्द्रुतम् ।
> काली करालवदना विनिष्क्रान्तासिपाशिनी ॥

*bhrukuṭīkuṭilāt tasyā lalāṭaphalakād drutam |*
*kālī karālavadanā viniṣkrāntāsipāśinī ||*

**English:** From the knitted, frowning brow of her forehead there swiftly came forth Kālī, terrible of face, bearing sword and noose.

**हिंदी:** उनके (अम्बिका के) ललाट-पटल की टेढ़ी भृकुटि से शीघ्र ही भयंकर मुख वाली, खड्ग और पाश धारण करने वाली काली प्रकट हुईं।

| | |
|---|---|
| **Text** | Devī Māhātmya (Durgā Saptaśatī), chapter 7 ("Caṇḍa-Muṇḍa-vadha"), verse 5. The Devī Māhātmya is part of the Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa. |
| **Read at** | [mantra.tips, Durgā Saptaśatī 7.5](https://mantra.tips/en/durga-saptashati/chapter-7/verse-5) |
| **Edition** | The site does not name one. Grade **A‑w**. Before publishing, check the number against the Gita Press *Śrīdurgāsaptaśatī*, where the chapter-7 numbering is standard. |
| **Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa numbering** | Chapter 7 of the Devī Māhātmya corresponds to MkP adhyāya 87 in the vedapath arrangement. That chapter number came from a summary of the page, and the verse number within MkP 87 is not yet verified. ⚠ |
| **Context** | Ambikā grows angry at the demons Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa, and Kālī springs from her brow. |

**Why this verse / यह श्लोक क्यों:** it is the scriptural moment of Kālī's appearance, and it comes from the Devī Māhātmya, one of the most stable and widely printed Śākta texts. It is the strongest-sourced Kālī verse we have.

## 2. Baṭuka Bhairava: sāttvika dhyāna / बटुक भैरव: सात्त्विक ध्यान

> वन्दे बालं स्फटिकसदृशं कुण्डलोद्भासिवक्त्रं
> दिव्याकल्पैर्नवमणिमयैः किङ्किणीनूपुराढ्यैः ।
> दीप्ताकारं विशदवदनं सुप्रसन्नं त्रिनेत्रं
> हस्ताब्जाभ्यां बटुकमनिशं शूलदण्डौ दधानम् ॥

*vande bālaṃ sphaṭikasadṛśaṃ kuṇḍalodbhāsivaktraṃ*
*divyākalpair navamaṇimayaiḥ kiṅkiṇīnūpurāḍhyaiḥ |*
*dīptākāraṃ viśadavadanaṃ suprasannaṃ trinetraṃ*
*hastābjābhyāṃ baṭukam aniśaṃ śūladaṇḍau dadhānam ||*

**English:** I bow to Baṭuka, the child, clear as crystal, his face shining with earrings, adorned with divine ornaments of the nine gems and with tinkling bells and anklets; radiant in form, bright-faced, gracious, three-eyed, holding always the trident and the staff in his two lotus hands.

**हिंदी:** मैं उन बालरूप बटुक को प्रणाम करता हूँ, जो स्फटिक के समान निर्मल हैं, जिनका मुख कुण्डलों से दमकता है, जो नवरत्नों से बने दिव्य आभूषणों तथा किंकिणी और नूपुरों से सुशोभित हैं; जो दीप्त-रूप, उज्ज्वल-मुख, अत्यन्त प्रसन्न और त्रिनेत्र हैं, और अपने कर-कमलों में सदा त्रिशूल और दण्ड धारण करते हैं।

**Grade: D ⚠ (traditional dhyāna; printed source not yet checked).** The verse is real and widely used. Three different works are named around it: the Mantramahārṇava, the Rudrayāmala and the Viśvasāroddhāra Tantra. We have not yet seen it in a printed edition of any of them.

**Suggested credit line for the site:** "Baṭuka Bhairava sāttvika dhyāna, as given with the Āpaduddhāraka Baṭuka Bhairava stotra (attributed to the Rudrayāmala; dhyāna cited from the Mantramahārṇava)."

## 3. Recommendation / सुझाव

The dedication page can carry both verses. **Kālī (Saptaśatī 7.5)** is grade A‑w. **Baṭuka (sāttvika dhyāna)** is grade D. Each verse should appear with its source line exactly as written above. The Baṭuka verse should not be credited to one Tantra alone, because three different works are named in the sources.
