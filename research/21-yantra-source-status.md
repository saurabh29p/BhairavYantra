# 21 · Baṭuka / Kāla Bhairava Yantra: Source Status / यन्त्र: स्रोत-स्थिति

*Last updated: 2026-11-15*

## Result / परिणाम

**EN:** We have **not yet found** a textual description of the Baṭuka Bhairava or Kāla Bhairava yantra (its layers, number of petals and bīja placement) that we could read and cite. Drawing a "Bhairava yantra" now would mean copying an unsourced image from the web, or composing one ourselves. Both go against this project's rule. **Recommendation: until the source is read, the website's frame should not be labelled or presented as a Bhairava yantra.** A plain decorative border is fine as long as it makes no claim to be a yantra.

**HI:** बटुक भैरव या काल भैरव यन्त्र का ऐसा ग्रंथीय विवरण (आवरण, दलों की संख्या, बीजों का स्थान) अभी तक हमें **नहीं मिला** जिसे पढ़कर उद्धृत किया जा सके। अभी "भैरव यन्त्र" बनाना या तो वेब से बिना स्रोत की छवि की नकल होगी या हमारी अपनी रचना, और दोनों इस परियोजना के नियम के विरुद्ध हैं। **सुझाव: जब तक स्रोत न पढ़ा जाए, वेबसाइट के फ्रेम को भैरव यन्त्र न कहा जाए।** साधारण सजावटी किनारी ठीक है, यदि वह यन्त्र होने का दावा न करे।

## What we know about where it is described / स्रोत कहाँ है, इसके संकेत

| Lead | What we found | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| **Mantramahodadhi** of Mahīdhara (25 taraṅgas; date: see note below) | The preface of the Hindi edition on archive.org ("Mantra Mahodadhi", Hindi commentary by Sudhākar Mālavīya) summarises **taraṅga 20** as covering **Svarṇākarṣaṇa Bhairava and yantras**. The Sanskrit OCR index lists a **Baṭuka bali-mantra** in taraṅga 10 and "अष्टभैरवकथनम्" (the eight Bhairavas) in taraṅga 5. The body text could not be read online. | B (from the index and preface only) |
| **Śāradātilaka** | Wikipedia's [Batuka Bhairava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batuka_Bhairava) says it prescribes sāttvika, rājasa and tāmasa forms. No yantra was confirmed. | C |
| **Rūpamaṇḍana** | Per Wikipedia (citing Gopinatha Rao, p. 177): eight-armed Baṭuka holding khaṭvāṅga, noose, trident, ḍamaru, skull-cup and snake, plus flesh and abhaya, with a dog of the same colour. This is iconography, not a yantra. | C |
| Gopinatha Rao, *Elements of Hindu Iconography* II.1, pp. 177–179 (Vaṭuka) and 179 (Svarṇākarṣaṇa) | The pages are known from the table of contents, but the text was not reachable. | — |

**Note on the date:** the Hindi edition's preface dates the work "1645". That is most likely Vikrama Saṃvat 1645, which is 1588/89 CE, the date usually given for the Mantramahodadhi. ⚠ To confirm.

## What would unblock this / आगे क्या चाहिए

Any one of these would be enough:
1. A PDF of the **Mantramahodadhi** (Sanskrit, or Sanskrit with Hindi), uploaded to the project. Taraṅgas 10 and 20 are the ones to read.
2. A PDF of Gopinatha Rao, *Elements of Hindu Iconography*, Vol. II Part I.
3. Network access in the cloud environment to `archive.org` (see the message in the research thread).
