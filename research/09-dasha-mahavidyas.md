## 1. The standard ten / मानक दस

The list most often given in modern scholarship, following David Kinsley, *Tantric Visions of the Divine Feminine: The Ten Mahāvidyās* (1997), pp. ix, 1, as cited on [Wikipedia, "Mahavidya"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahavidya). Grade **C** (scholarly list). It still needs to be traced to a primary text with chapter and verse.

| # | IAST | देवनागरी |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kālī | काली |
| 2 | Tārā | तारा |
| 3 | Tripurasundarī (Ṣoḍaśī) | त्रिपुरसुन्दरी (षोडशी) |
| 4 | Bhuvaneśvarī | भुवनेश्वरी |
| 5 | Bhairavī | भैरवी |
| 6 | Chinnamastā | छिन्नमस्ता |
| 7 | Dhūmāvatī | धूमावती |
| 8 | Bagalāmukhī | बगलामुखी |
| 9 | Mātaṅgī | मातङ्गी |
| 10 | Kamalā (Kamalātmikā) | कमला (कमलात्मिका) |

⚠ The **order varies** between texts, and so does how Ṣoḍaśī relates to Tripurasundarī. Wikipedia, citing Kinsley's *Hindu Goddesses* (1987) pp. 161–165, says the Mahābhāgavata and Bṛhaddharma Purāṇas identify Ṣoḍaśī with Tripurasundarī. This has not yet been checked in those texts.

## 2. Primary-text witnesses found so far / अब तक मिले ग्रंथीय साक्ष्य

### Devī Bhāgavata Purāṇa 7.28 (Śatākṣī / Śākambharī episode)
- Source: [Wisdom Library, DBP Book 7 ch. 28](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/devi-bhagavata-purana/d/doc57285.html), English translation by Swami Vijñānānanda (1921–23). Grade **B** (English only; Sanskrit not yet checked).
- In the battle with the demon Durgama, Śaktis come out of the Devī's body. The translation names: Kālikā, Tāriṇī, Ṣoḍaśī, Tripurā, Bhairavī, Kamalā, Bagalā, Mātaṅgī, Tripurasundarī, Kāmākṣī, Tulajā Devī, Jambhinī, Mohinī and Chinnamastā, then Guhyakālīs with ten thousand arms.
- **Note:** this is **not** a list of ten. It names Ṣoḍaśī and Tripurasundarī **separately**. It does not include Bhuvaneśvarī or Dhūmāvatī. ⚠

### Mahābhāgavata Purāṇa ch. 8 (Satī shows ten forms to Śiva)
- This is the best-known origin story. Satī, refused permission by Śiva to attend Dakṣa's sacrifice, manifests ten forms that surround him in the ten directions.
- **Not yet verified.** We could not reach the text online. Edition to consult: the Mahābhāgavata Purāṇa (Devīpurāṇa) edited by Pushpendra Kumar (Delhi: Eastern Book Linkers, 1983) *(edition details from memory, to confirm)*. Kinsley 1997 discusses the episode in his introductory chapter. ⚠

### Bṛhaddharma Purāṇa, Madhyakhaṇḍa ch. 6
- A parallel version of the Satī episode. **Not yet verified.** ⚠

## 3. Mahāvidyās and the ten avatāras of Viṣṇu / दशावतार से संबंध

Wikipedia attributes to the **Toḍala Tantra, ch. 10** the following mapping, but tags it "citation needed". Grade **D ⚠** until checked.

| Tripurasundarī | Paraśurāma |
| Bhuvaneśvarī | Vāmana |
