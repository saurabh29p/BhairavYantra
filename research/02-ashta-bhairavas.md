# 02 · The Aṣṭa Bhairavas (Eight Bhairavas) / अष्ट भैरव

*Last updated: 2026-11-08 · Status: **DRAFT — primary source not yet located.** Grades follow [00-methodology-and-sources.md](../docs/01-methodology-and-sources.md).*

## Status in one paragraph / स्थिति

**EN:** The eight names below are consistent across every source we have read. However, **we have not yet found them in a primary text with chapter and verse.** They are **not** in the Kāśīkhaṇḍa (Pūrvārdha ch. 30–31) or in Śiva Purāṇa Śatarudra Saṃhitā ch. 8–9 (see file 01). Popular websites and Wikipedia attribute the list, the 64 subordinate Bhairavas, and the colours and vehicles to "Rudrayāmala" or "Śiva Āgamas", but give no chapter or verse. The details they give (colours, vehicles, associated gods) **contradict each other**. The standard scholarly source is T. A. Gopinatha Rao, *Elements of Hindu Iconography*, Vol. II Part I (1916), pp. 174–182. We have confirmed from its table of contents that it treats "Bhairava", "Vaṭuka-Bhairava", "Svarṇākarṣaṇa-Bhairava" and "Sixty-four Bhairavas" there, but the page text could not yet be retrieved.

**HI:** नीचे दिए आठ नाम हमारे पढ़े सभी स्रोतों में एक जैसे हैं। पर **अभी तक हमें ये किसी मूल ग्रंथ में अध्याय-श्लोक सहित नहीं मिले।** ये काशीखण्ड (पूर्वार्ध अ. ३०–३१) और शिव पुराण शतरुद्र संहिता (अ. ८–९) में **नहीं** हैं। लोकप्रिय वेबसाइटें और विकिपीडिया इस सूची, ६४ भैरवों, तथा रंग-वाहन आदि को "रुद्रयामल" या "शिवागम" से बताती हैं, पर अध्याय-श्लोक नहीं देतीं, और उनके विवरण **आपस में विरोधी** हैं। मानक विद्वत् स्रोत टी. ए. गोपीनाथ राव, *Elements of Hindu Iconography*, खंड २ भाग १ (१९१६), पृ. १७४–१८२ है; इसकी विषय-सूची से पुष्टि हुई है कि वहाँ "भैरव", "वटुक-भैरव", "स्वर्णाकर्षण-भैरव" और "चौंसठ भैरव" पर खंड हैं, पर पृष्ठों का पाठ अभी प्राप्त नहीं हो सका।

## 1. The listing verse / नाम-सूची श्लोक

> असिताङ्गो रुरुश्चण्डः क्रोधश्चोन्मत्तभैरवः ।
> कपाली भीषणश्चैव संहारश्चाष्टभैरवम् ॥

*asitāṅgo ruruś caṇḍaḥ krodhaś conmattabhairavaḥ |*
*kapālī bhīṣaṇaś caiva saṃhāraś cāṣṭabhairavam ||*

**EN (ours):** Asitāṅga, Ruru, Caṇḍa, Krodha, Unmatta-bhairava, Kapālī, Bhīṣaṇa and Saṃhāra: these are the eight Bhairavas.
**HI (हमारा):** असिताङ्ग, रुरु, चण्ड, क्रोध, उन्मत्त भैरव, कपाली, भीषण और संहार — ये आठ भैरव हैं।

- **Where read:** [pavitragranth.com, Aṣṭabhairava Dhyānastotram](https://pavitragranth.com/stotras/ashta-bhairava-dhyana-stotram) and [ptvaishnavi.blogspot.com](https://ptvaishnavi.blogspot.com/2021/07/Ashta-bhairav.html). Both pages carry the same text with small copying differences, so they are not independent witnesses.
- **Source stated by those pages:** none. pavitragranth says only "मूल पाठ: पारंपरिक धार्मिक स्रोत" ("root text: traditional religious source").
- **Grade: D ⚠** until located in a printed Tantra or Purāṇa.

## 2. Comparative table: what secondary sources claim / द्वितीयक स्रोतों के दावे

None of these columns is yet confirmed from a primary text. Where two sources differ, both are shown as "A / B".

**Sources:** **W** = [Wikipedia, "Ashta Bhairava"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashta_Bhairava) (cites Gopinatha Rao 1914 p. 28 and Payne & Witzel 2015, *Homa Variations*, p. 330, without saying which claim each supports). **N** = [nathas.org dictionary, "Ashta-bhairava"](https://nathas.org/en/dictionary/ashta-bhairava/) (cites "Śiva Āgamas", "Rudrayāmala" and the Śrīvidyā "Mahāṣoḍhā-nyāsa", without verse).

| # | Name | नाम | Direction (W, N agree) | Consort (Mātṛkā) | Vehicle | Colour (W / N) | Weapons (W only) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Asitāṅga | असिताङ्ग | East / पूर्व | Brāhmī | Swan (haṃsa) | white / navy | rosary, water-pot, sword, skull-cup |
| 2 | Ruru | रुरु | South-east / आग्नेय | Māheśvarī | Bull | light blue / snow white | deer, axe, sword, bowl |
| 3 | Caṇḍa | चण्ड | South / दक्षिण | Kaumārī | Peacock | white / gold | bow, arrow, sword, bowl |
| 4 | Krodha | क्रोध | South-west / नैर्ऋत्य | Vaiṣṇavī | Garuḍa (eagle) | dark blue / black | conch, discus, mace, bowl |
| 5 | Unmatta | उन्मत्त | West / पश्चिम | Vārāhī | Horse | golden / gold | sword, skull-cup, pestle, shield |
| 6 | Kapāla | कपाल | North-west / वायव्य | Indrāṇī (Aindrī) | Elephant | shining yellow / pink | vajra, noose, sword, bowl |
| 7 | Bhīṣaṇa | भीषण | North / उत्तर | Cāmuṇḍā | Preta (W) / Lion (N) ⚠ | blood red / red | sword, skull-cup, trident, pestle |
| 8 | Saṃhāra | संहार | North-east / ईशान | Nārasiṃhī (W) / Mahālakṣmī, Nārasiṃhī or Yogeśvarī (N) ⚠ | Lion (W) / Dog (N) ⚠ | yellow-orange / white | 10 arms: trident, drum, conch, mace, discus, sword, bowl, khaṭvāṅga, noose, goad |

**Observations / टिप्पणियाँ:**
- The **names and directions** agree in all sources read.
- The **Mātṛkā pairings** follow the standard order of the Seven/Eight Mothers. This fits a known Tantric pattern, but the pairing table itself is still unsourced.
- The **associated deities** differ completely. W gives Viṣṇu, Brahmā, Sūrya, Śiva, Indra, Candra, Yama. N's table gives the eight dikpālas (Indra, Agni, Yama, Nirṛti, Varuṇa, Vāyu, Kubera, Īśāna), while N's own prose matches W. ⚠
- An eighth Mātṛkā for Saṃhāra varies, because Mātṛkā lists themselves vary between seven and eight. ⚠

## 3. Dhyāna ślokas (meditation verses) / ध्यान श्लोक

A set of eight dhyāna verses, one per Bhairava, circulates as the *Aṣṭabhairava Dhyānastotram*. Opening lines as read at [pavitragranth.com](https://pavitragranth.com/stotras/ashta-bhairava-dhyana-stotram):

| # | Bhairava | Opening pāda (verbatim) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | असिताङ्ग | रक्तज्वालजटाधरं शशियुतं रक्ताङ्ग तेजोमयं … |
| 2 | रुरु | निर्वाणं निर्विकल्पं निरूपजमलं निर्विकारं … |
| 3 | चण्ड | बिभ्राणं शुभ्रवर्णं द्विगुणदशभुजं पञ्चवक्त्रन्त्रिनेत्रं … |
| 4 | क्रोध | उद्यद्भास्कररूपनिभन्त्रिनयनं रक्ताङ्ग रागाम्बुजं … |
| 5 | उन्मत्त | एकं खट्वाङ्गहस्तं पुनरपि भुजगं पाशमेकन्त्रिशूलं … |
| 6 | कपाल | वन्दे बालं स्फटिकसदृशं कुम्भलोल्लासिवक्त्रं … |
| 7 | भीषण | त्रिनेत्रं रक्तवर्णञ्च सर्वाभरणभूषितम् … |
| 8 | संहार | एकवक्त्रन्त्रिनेत्रञ्च हस्तयो द्वादशन्तथा … |

⚠ **Grade D.** No source text is named. The online text has visible copying errors (e.g., "शुनवाहनन्त्रिनयन"), so the full verses will be given only after we find a printed edition to check them against. Note also that these dhyānas do **not** match the colours in §2. For example, the Asitāṅga dhyāna says "red-limbed" (रक्ताङ्ग), while W says white and N says navy. The Caṇḍa dhyāna gives 20 arms and 5 faces (द्विगुणदशभुजं पञ्चवक्त्र), while W gives four arms. And the Saṃhāra dhyāna gives 12 hands, while W gives ten.

**Added 2026-11-15:** the "Kapāla" dhyāna in this set (वन्दे बालं स्फटिकसदृशं…) is the same verse that is given elsewhere as the **sāttvika dhyāna of Baṭuka Bhairava**. The "Krodha" dhyāna (उद्यद्भास्कररूपनिभं…) closely matches the opening of Baṭuka's **rājasa dhyāna** (उद्यद्भास्करसन्निभं…). See [20-dedication-verses.md](20-dedication-verses.md). So this circulating Aṣṭa Bhairava set seems to reuse Baṭuka dhyānas, which is one more reason to treat it as unverified. ⚠

## 4. Other lists of eight that differ / भिन्न सूचियाँ

- A reader's comment on [manblunder.com](https://manblunder.com/articlesview/forms-of-bhairava) gives a completely different set of eight names, spelled as posted: "Ekapatha Bhairava, Kala Bhaira, Karaalaga Bhairava, marupa Bhairava, Hethuka Bhairava, Thiripura Bhairava, Agni Bhairava, Jamajikava Bhairava". This is unverified (grade D ⚠). Our hypothesis, not yet checked: these look like Ekapāda, Kāla, Karāla, Bhīmarūpa(?), Hetuka, Tripurāntaka, Agnijihva(?) and Jvālājihva(?), names that we recall from Tantric lists of **Kṣetrapālas** rather than Aṣṭa Bhairavas. To investigate.
- [Wikipedia, "Bhairava"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhairava) cites the standard eight to the *Kubjikā Upaniṣad* (Goudriaan & Schoterman, Egbert Forsten), p. 7. To check.
